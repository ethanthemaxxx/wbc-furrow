Groundwork first.
Slow where it counts, fast everywhere else.

Four phases. The first two take about a month, and you see numbers in week three.

  • Phase 01

    Audit the ground

    We read your store, ads, email and unit economics. You get a margin map and a ranked list of fixes.

  • Phase 02

    Plant the first tests

    We launch the three highest-profit tests across store, ads and email, with a clear stop rule for each.

  • Phase 03

    Tend what works

    Weekly reads, monthly creative, quarterly review. We cut losers early and feed the winners.

  • Phase 04

    Scale what holds

    Budgets go up only where payback stays inside your target. Playbooks go to your team.

What stays constant
from week one

  • One owner. A named strategist who reads your numbers every week.
  • Open books. Dashboards you can see, with every test and its result logged.
  • Your playbooks. When the work ends, the documents stay with you.
