How Fernhill kept more of every order

The challenge

Orders were up 60% in a year.* Profit per order was down.

Fernhill spent most of its budget on one broad Meta campaign and offered free shipping on every order. Product cost and fulfillment fees rose with volume, and the team judged channels on ROAS. When we looked at contribution margin by campaign, three of the five biggest ones were below break-even after the first order.

Our approach

Fix the order first. Then buy more of it.

We started with the store. A bundle on the product page and a shipping threshold moved AOV from $48 to $61*, which gave the ads room. Then we rebuilt the ad plan around payback: fewer broad campaigns, more specific creative for each product line. Last, we rewrote the post-purchase and replenishment flows so a second order didn’t cost another ad.

The results

Over nine months, contribution margin rose from 31% to 44%*. CAC payback fell from 94 days to 51*. Repeat purchase rate moved from 22% to 34%*, and email and SMS reached a quarter of revenue*. Fernhill spent less on its three weakest campaigns and more on the two that earned it back.
